Private CRM · Privacy notice

How this CRM uses connected account data

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Operator and purpose

Kasra Aliyon operates this private Twenty CRM instance for professional relationship management. Questions or deletion requests can be sent to kasra@7scholar.com.

Information accessed

With your authorization, Google access includes your account identity and email addresses, Gmail messages and metadata, drafts, and calendar events. The requested permissions support reading mail, composing and sending messages, and reading and managing events. Microsoft connections provide the corresponding information and actions covered by your consent. The CRM also stores contact and company records, notes, tasks, uploaded files, and information you capture or synchronize through its LinkedIn extension.

Use and sharing

Connected account information is used to provide the CRM features you enable: correspondence and calendar synchronization, contact history, search, drafting, sending, and authorized workflows. Workspace sharing follows the access and visibility settings configured in the CRM.

The service uses a Contabo server for hosting and keeps protected backup copies. Configured integration providers receive information needed for the features you authorize. If you authorize an AI assistant through the CRM or MCP, that assistant and its service provider may process the records required for your request, subject to its permissions and your configuration.

Google user data must not be sold, used for personalized advertising, or used to train general-purpose AI models. The operator must configure connected services consistently with these restrictions. Google user data is used and transferred only to provide the user-facing CRM features described here and in accordance with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage, security, and retention

Production connections use HTTPS. The CRM encrypts stored OAuth credentials, restricts access by workspace permissions, and maintains encrypted off-server backups. Records remain until deleted or no longer needed for the configured CRM purpose. Deleted information may remain in retained backups until those backups expire or are removed. Backup retention is controlled by the operator.

Your controls

You can disconnect an account in the CRM and revoke Google access at Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized API access but does not automatically erase records already synchronized. To request removal of those records or discuss backup retention, contact the operator. Connecting additional services is optional.
