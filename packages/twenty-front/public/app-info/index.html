Private CRM · App information

Twenty CRM for 7Scholar

This private, self-hosted CRM is operated by Kasra Aliyon for managing professional contacts, companies, tasks, correspondence, and follow-ups. Access is limited to authorized workspace members.

Connected accounts

When you connect a Google or Microsoft account, the CRM can display authorized email and calendar information alongside your contact records. It supports searching correspondence, drafting and sending messages, and managing calendar events according to the permissions you grant and the actions or workflows you authorize.

An optional LinkedIn browser extension captures and synchronizes authorized LinkedIn information. Optional AI tools can work with CRM records within the access you grant them.

Read the privacy notice and use terms before connecting an account.
